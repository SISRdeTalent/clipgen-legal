Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. About the app

GTAVI CLIP is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by its developer to prepare short gaming video clips and upload them to the developer's own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to the public.

2. Use of TikTok

The app only acts on the TikTok account that explicitly authorizes it through TikTok Login Kit. It uploads videos to that account (as drafts or posts) using the TikTok Content Posting API, and complies with the TikTok Terms of Service and Developer Terms.

3. Content

Clips are credited to their original creators. Any creator who wants a clip removed can request it and it will be removed promptly. GTAVI CLIP is not affiliated with Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, TikTok or Google.

The app is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind.
